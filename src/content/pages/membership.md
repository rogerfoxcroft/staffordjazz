<p>Although not a membership-only organisation, Stafford Jazz Society welcomes membership and offers benefits for membership.  SJS Membership runs annually from 1<sup>st</sup> July to 30 June the following year. Members receive a personal membership card, have the opportunity to stand for Committee and to participate and vote in the Society’s Annual General Meeting. There is a financial incentive to becoming a member.  Concert admission charges are £8 (Members) and £9 (Non-Members).</p>
<p>Annual Subscription Rates are currently:</p>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Individual Membership per person: £10</li>
<li>Under 21s:&nbsp; £5</li>
</ul>
