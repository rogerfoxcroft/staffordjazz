<p>There was a little confusion regarding the<br />
first concert of the season, Chris Pearce&rsquo;s Frenchmen Street Jazz Band, as we<br />
thought it would be a lunchtime gig and it ended up as an evening concert due<br />
to the darts.  We did try to spread the<br />
word as far and wide as possible but the audience was perhaps not as full as it<br />
might have been.  However that made no<br />
difference to the sheer enjoyment of this group, who played their socks off.</p>
<p>Next came Matt Palmer&rsquo;s Millennium Eagle<br />
Jazz Band and it is always a pleasure to hear Matt, who is one of the UK&rsquo;s<br />
brightest jazz talents.   On 20 September<br />
we heard Original Panama Jazz Band who had a good repertoire with numbers old<br />
and new.  They played with gusto and<br />
skill, along with the odd joke, and time simply flew past.   The last concert in September was Chris<br />
Carmell&rsquo;s Vintage Jazzmen with their 20&rsquo;s style jazz.</p>
<p>The first band in October was the Savannah<br />
Jazz Band (see picture below).   Along<br />
with a good programme they kept up a steady stream of humorous comments and<br />
reminiscences.  There was amazing mouth<br />
organ playing by the reeds player, Bill Smith, which was absolute heaven.</p>
<figure data-orig-width="774" data-orig-height="530" class="tmblr-full"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_nwzmfovMdX1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-width="774" data-orig-height="530" /></figure>
<p>On 11 October it was a delight 5to see<br />
Carole Westwood, newly married, back on our stage.  She was accompanied by a trio of musicians<br />
(John Evans on saxophone, Robert Armstrong on drums and John Evans on<br />
keyboard).  There were several pieces<br />
during the gig with a shoulder-twitching upbeat.  By golly could that John Evans play.</p>
<figure data-orig-width="4320" data-orig-height="3240" class="tmblr-full"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_nwzjvcDGiJ1rp3l2g_540.jpg" alt="image" data-orig-width="4320" data-orig-height="3240" /></figure>
<p>On 17 October we welcomed Kevin Grenfell&rsquo;s<br />
Jazz Giants.   This was good music with<br />
everything from mood music to boogie woogie, all played with consummate<br />
skill.   There were jokes a plenty from<br />
Kevin.  What a programme.  As always the audience showed their<br />
appreciation with a standing ovation.</p>
<figure data-orig-width="4320" data-orig-height="3240" class="tmblr-full"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_nwzjvdlCdx1rp3l2g_540.jpg" alt="image" data-orig-width="4320" data-orig-height="3240" /></figure>

birthday.  Heather is a regular supporter<br />
and here she lead the way in the umbrella parade at a gallop.</p>
