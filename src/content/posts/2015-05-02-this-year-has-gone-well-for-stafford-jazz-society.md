<p>This year has gone well for Stafford Jazz Society.   The groups have been in tremendous form and<br />
audiences are growing week by week.</p>
<p>January saw Apex Jazz &amp; Swing Band, the JB Jazz &amp;<br />
Blues Band, George Huxley&rsquo;s Hot Five and the Old Fashioned Love Band.  Tiggy&rsquo;s kitchen branched out to include chips<br />
in the goodies available to buy – what a treat when the weather is cold.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="305" data-orig-width="405"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_nnpdr6dkMI1rp3l2g_540.png" data-orig-height="305" data-orig-width="405" /></figure>
<p>Martin showing off his wares</p>
<p>February saw the<br />
reappearance of the ever popular Swingin&rsquo; Jim&rsquo;s Dance Band.  Annie Hawkins Jazzmen is always a welcome<br />
sight on the stage and Martin Bennett&rsquo;s Swamplanders played with a vigour which<br />
belied their age.  Matt Palmer&rsquo;s<br />
Millennium Eagle Jazz Band saw February draw to a close.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="273" data-orig-width="400"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_nnpdtcKFi61rp3l2g_540.png" data-orig-height="273" data-orig-width="400" /></figure>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="303" data-orig-width="403"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_nnpdtlGx1W1rp3l2g_540.png" data-orig-height="303" data-orig-width="403" /></figure>
<p>Everybody joins in the Brolly Parade (Ken Probert showing his<br />
mobile brolly skills)</p>
<p>March brought us Steve King Big Band, Funky Butt Hall<br />
N’Awlins Jazzband (don&rsquo;t you just love the way you say N’Awlins &hellip; it is only<br />
the uninitiated that call it New Orleans!), Eric &ldquo;Busker&rsquo; Newton&rsquo;s Willys<br />
Weepers. Chris Pearce&rsquo;s Frenchmen Street Jazz Band and The Phoenix Jazzmen.</p>
<p>Tables had been decorated with vases of daffodils in honour<br />
of Mothering Sunday so the room looked unusually bright and cheerful.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="308" data-orig-width="410"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_nnpdv4YHwB1rp3l2g_540.png" data-orig-height="308" data-orig-width="410" /></figure>

<p>April brought Chris Carmell&rsquo;s Vintage Jazz Band, and Mart<br />
Rodger Manchester Jazz and Jeff Milner&rsquo;s Delta Friends.   Again for Easter the tables held vases of<br />
spring flowers and some had even brought along Easter bonnets for the brolly<br />
parade.  The second evening concert of<br />
the season was taken by New Orleans Heat on 26 May.</p>
