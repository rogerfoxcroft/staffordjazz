<p><strong>27 October</strong> – Merseysippi Jazz Band</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Picture-1.webp" alt="" class="wp-image-252"/></figure>
<p><strong>20 October – Baby Jools &amp; the Jazzaholics:</strong>&nbsp;&nbsp;This group were playing today to celebrate SJS 38<sup>th</sup>&nbsp;Anniversary Concert.&nbsp;&nbsp;The band consisted of Baby Jools on drums (leader), Jim Swinnerton on bass, Brian Mellor on banjo, Mike Owen on trombone, Danny Illet on trumpet and Karl Hird on reeds.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This band are simply superb with music including:&nbsp;&nbsp;‘Girl of My Dreams’, ‘Old Man Moses’ (my favourite of the day), ‘Mama’s Gone to Buy’, ‘Since I laid my Burden Down’, ‘Petite Fleur’, ‘Those were the Days’ and ‘When My Dreamboat comes Home.&nbsp;&nbsp;Feet were tapping and everyone left with smiles on their faces.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;What a great gig.</p>
<p><strong>13 October</strong>&nbsp;– No Concert</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Picture-2.webp" alt="" class="wp-image-253"/></figure>
<p><strong>6 October – Wabash Jazzmen:</strong>&nbsp;&nbsp;A highly entertaining band with a distinctive rhythmic style influenced by early Ken Colyer and Chris Barber bands.&nbsp;&nbsp;Playing today were Mark Challinor (leader) on banjo/guitar/ukulele, Richard Vernon on string bass, Mike Pemberton on trombone, Bill Smith on cornet and harmonica and Matt Palmer, standing in for Mike Hayler currently sick, playing the reeds.&nbsp;&nbsp;Their opening number was ‘Curse of an Aching Heart’.&nbsp;&nbsp;Some of the other numbers played during this gig were: ‘Savoy Blues’, ‘I’m Doing Nothing’, ‘Blue turning Grey over You’, ‘Fidgetty Feet’, ‘You Meet the Nicest People in Your Dreams’ and ‘East Coast Trot’.&nbsp;&nbsp;Also played, a number first recorded by the Band twenty five years ago, was ‘The Fat Cat on a Fence’.&nbsp;&nbsp;They finished up with ‘TheWolverine Blues’.&nbsp;&nbsp;I don’t need to say it but I will, the Band were very well received as they are popular with Stafford Jazz Society and their programme and music is great.&nbsp;&nbsp;Look forward to next time.</p>
