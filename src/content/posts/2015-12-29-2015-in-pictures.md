<div class="wp-content">
<p>On 25 October we had another chance to see<br />
and hear the Chicago Teddybears Society Jazz Band.  Despite roadworks on the road approaching the<br />
White Eagle Club, nothing stopped the audience from pouring in for this<br />
concert.   Not surprisingly they were the<br />
winner of the Band of the year Trophy Award, and were presentd with their<br />
awards by Jenni and Ian D’Arcy.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="590" data-orig-width="894"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_o049dt5OVn1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-height="590" data-orig-width="894" /></figure>
<p>1st November gave us Baby Jools &amp; The<br />
Jazzaholics.   There was an exciting<br />
programme of music with good pace.   Baby<br />
Jools is a virtuoso drummer and a bright star in New Orleans style jazz in the<br />
UK.   The fact that this was an evening<br />
programme might account for a smaller than anticipated audience.  However those of us present enjoyed a super<br />
night of jazz.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="588" data-orig-width="831"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_o049ecAxVK1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-height="588" data-orig-width="831" /></figure>
<p>Brian Carrick&rsquo;s Algiers Stompers played on<br />
8 November.  This is a fine bunch of<br />
musicians and Brian Carrick not only plays the sax but has a pretty good voice<br />
too.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="592" data-orig-width="872"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_o049f5vVo51rp3l2g_540.png" data-orig-height="592" data-orig-width="872" /></figure>
<p>The talented Matt Palmer was with us once<br />
more on 15 November with his Aces of Rhythm.<br />
This group specialised in reviving jazz from the 1920s and so brings<br />
little known music to a modern audience.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="561" data-orig-width="872"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_o049g3gTV81rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-height="561" data-orig-width="872" /></figure>
<p>22 November gave us Ron Hills&rsquo; Swing Kings with<br />
Terry Roberts on reeds, Roger Heeley on keyboard, Roy Dutton on drums, Denis<br />
Mowatt on double bass, Dave Leithead on trumpet and Ron Hills on trombone.  Just in case you were wondering, the little<br />
white ghost centre stage is Roy Dutton!</p>
<p>Sadly I missed the JB Jazz &amp; Blues Band<br />
on 29 November,due to another commitment on the same day.  Groan!<br />
They are such a good band and give a wonderful performance.  Which leads us into December.  On 6th we had the Phoenix Jazzmen with Sheila<br />
Fawkes.  My word can that little lady<br />
sing.  She has one of the best jazz<br />
voices around.  See picture below.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="570" data-orig-width="882"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_o049gsDwo51rp3l2g_540.png" data-orig-height="570" data-orig-width="882" /></figure>
<p>The last band of the year, on 6 December,<br />
was Bev Pegg &amp; His Good Time Jazz Gang.<br />
Again sadly I missed this so cannot report on it from my own personal<br />
view.  However I understand that they<br />
went down a storm.</p>
<p>My final picture of the year shows  Gill Dodds being presented with the SJS<br />
Member of the Year Trophy.   Jill has<br />
been coming to the jazz for a long time.<br />
This year health problems meant that she had to miss quite a few<br />
shows.   I am delighted to report she has<br />
made a good recovery and was so surprised and delighted to be nominated for the<br />
Award.   Seen hers presenting the trophy<br />
is our Vice-Chairman and Membership Secretary, Shirley Ollier.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="473" data-orig-width="407"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_o049h36uEa1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-height="473" data-orig-width="407" /></figure>
<p>We will be back for the first gig on 10<br />
January with Robin Mason&rsquo;s Apex Jazz &amp; Swing Band.  Looking forward to it already.</p>
