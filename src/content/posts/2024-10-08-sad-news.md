<p>Following the sad death and great loss of Jeremy Huggett, Stafford Jazz Society announces that the previously publicised JEREMY HUGGETT’S JAZZ &amp; BOOGIE BAND concerts scheduled for 4 November 2024 and 16 March 2025 will now be re-titled KEVIN GRENFELL’S JAZZ &amp; BOOGIE BAND.</p>
<p>Furthermore, John Hallam will now be on reeds for the KEVIN GRENFELL’S JAZZ GIANTS concert on 3 November 2024.</p>
