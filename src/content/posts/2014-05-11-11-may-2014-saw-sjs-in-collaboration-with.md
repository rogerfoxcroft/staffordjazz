<p>11 May 2014 saw SJS, in collaboration with Stafford Music Festival, hosting Marston&rsquo;s Pedigree Jazzs Band – &ldquo;Tribute to Trad Jazz&rdquo; – at the White Eagle Club.   The concert, which attracted an excellent audience, was well received with the audience enjoying the band&rsquo;s good music and good humour.   It was also a foretaste for members of things to come as the Society is due to move to the White Eagle Club as it&rsquo;s regular venue as from it&rsquo;s Christmas concert on 14 December.  </p>
