<p>The final show, on 18 December, was the Firehose 1 Dixieland<br />
Jazz Band.   They set the room on fire<br />
with their fire-fighting uniform and music.<br />
Dave Leighead led on trumpet with Dave Dixon on reeds, Richard Slater on<br />
trombone and sousaphone, Peter &ldquo;Pedro&rdquo; Robinson on banjo and guitar,<br />
Denis Goodwin on drums and Malcolm Hogarth on keyboard.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="601" data-orig-width="801"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_oj7q5fNj8n1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-height="601" data-orig-width="801" /></figure>
<p>Following the first half of the concert there was a presentation<br />
when the Member of the Year Award was passed by holder for 2016, Gill Dodd, to<br />
elected member, Joan Latham, who will be Member of the Year for 2017.  Congratulations to Joan.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="600" data-orig-width="800"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_oj7q7kpKmj1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-height="600" data-orig-width="800" /></figure>
<p>Thanks from the entire SJS are going to Shirley Ollier for<br />
the beautiful Christmas table decorations.<br />
The room looked lovely with little candle lights twinkling set on<br />
festive tables with red and green napkins.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="708" data-orig-width="621"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_oj7qalmqBk1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-height="708" data-orig-width="621" /></figure>
<p>The penultimate concert in 2016 was by JB Jazz &amp; Blues<br />
Band.   The band, with the addition of Ed<br />
Miller who replaced Mark Howell who sadly died earlier in the year, brought<br />
with it the &ldquo;feel good&rdquo; factor always associated with this band.  Other members of the band were John Beckingham<br />
on keyboard, Roger Mainwaring and Andy Loverage on reeds, Phil Probert on<br />
guitar and banjo and Paul Holder on drums.</p>

<p>The beginning of December saw Matt Palmer&rsquo;s Millennium Eagle<br />
Jazz Band.  This band always receives a<br />
consistently high rating from SJS members.<br />
The all star line-up was welcomed with Matt Palmer lead virtuoso<br />
reedsman, Terry Williams on the trombone, Pete Brown playing trumpet &amp;<br />
valve trombone, Baby Jools on drums, Brian Lawrence on double bass and Brian<br />
Mellor on banjo.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="931" data-orig-width="695"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_oj7qefLkgD1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-height="931" data-orig-width="695" /></figure>
<p>Chris Carmell&rsquo;s Vintage Jazzmen came on 20 November.  Especially remembered at this concert was the<br />
late and greatly missed Clinton Sedgley.<br />
Sadly I had no camera with me on this day and so no photograph was<br />
taken.  I promise to do better!</p>
<p>At the end of November we had Chris Pearce &amp; Andy<br />
Leggett&rsquo;s Sopranos.  This was a<br />
marvellous programme by a marvellous band.<br />
They have acquired a new trombonist, explaining that they needed a<br />
younger person to do all the lifting and carrying!!  I was particularly taken with a number called<br />
&lsquo;In My Darkest Hour&rsquo;, something slightly outside the general repertoire and a<br />
surprisingly cheerful number, given the title.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="602" data-orig-width="800"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_oj7qf7Quq71rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-height="602" data-orig-width="800" /></figure>
<p>On 13 November we had Ron Hills&rsquo; Swing Kings with Ron Hills<br />
on trombone, Tony Pipkin on trumpet and flugelhorn, Roger Heely, piano, Terry<br />
Roberts on reeds with Roy Dutton on drums, Denis Mowatt on bass and again we<br />
welcomed young Richard Hughes on keyboard.<br />
These are seriously good musicians and the band was well received at the<br />
White Eagle Club.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="600" data-orig-width="807"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_oj7qg3mSSO1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-height="600" data-orig-width="807" /></figure>
<p>The start of November saw Kevin Grenfell&rsquo;s Jazz Giants.   They played snappy, catchy music from the<br />
start but followed by a range of compositions.<br />
Richard Hughes was a welcome addition on keyboard.  I was once told to keep a close eye on the<br />
keyboard player as only very accomplished musicians used both hands, and this<br />
young man&rsquo;s hands were everywhere.  The<br />
rest of the band consisted of Kevin Grenfell on trumpet and trombone, Matt<br />
Palmer on reeds, Baby Jools on drums and Wayne Elliott on base guitar.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="556" data-orig-width="807"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_oj7qh6fsAs1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-height="556" data-orig-width="807" /></figure>
<p>The last concert in October was Carole Westwood&rsquo;s<br />
Sophisticated Swing.  We have not seen<br />
Carole for some time as she has been over in Australia.  Carole, as always, was in good voice and we<br />
are pleased she is back in England.   The<br />
music was varied but always with a beat to it.<br />
Some fabulous numbers were played by the Band, including Caravan and (my<br />
favourite!) My Baby Just Cares for Me.<br />
There was John Evans on keyboard, Terry Roberts on saxes and Roger<br />
Armstrong on drums.</p>
<figure class="tmblr-full" data-orig-height="626" data-orig-width="814"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_oj7qi3S4tl1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-height="626" data-orig-width="814" /></figure>
<p>On 23 October the SJS welcomed back the Chicago Teddybears<br />
Society Jazz Band to help celebrate the SJS Anniversary.   What a cracking good band this is and the<br />
crowd that turned out spoke for itself.<br />
Heads were nodding, feet were tapping and all enjoyed the music played<br />
by this band of high class musicians.<br />
Come back soon we all say.</p>
