<p>The snow blizzards and drifting throughout today (Friday) together with bleak weather forecasts of freezing temperatures for Saturday and of yet more snow expected on Sunday have forced Stafford Jazz Society to take the difficult decision to cancel this Sunday&rsquo;s (January 20th) concert.</p>
<p>Roads in the Midlands have already been badly hit with severe traffic problems resulting and even if the Band manage to get through – some need to travel a considerable distance – it is almost certain that audience numbers would be very badly down.</p>
<p>I regret any inconvenience and disappointment caused by this decision that is beyond our control.</p>
<p>Nick Balmforth<br />Chairman</p>
