<p>28 May – Ivory &amp; Gold (now renamed The Sweet &amp; Hot<br />
Quartet).  The final concert in the SJS<br />
2016/17 year.  This foursome is really<br />
the thinking man&rsquo;s jazz.  Great music –<br />
ranging from syncopated to blues and cakewalk and blues – and great fun.  Graham Smith (drums) gave his maiden vocal<br />
rendition which brought tears to the eyes!!<br />
Thomas &lsquo;Spats&rsquo; Langham (current SJS Musician of the year) was on banjo<br />
and guitar, Jeff Barnhart on piano and his wife Anne on flute.  I hope they are back with us soon.</p>
<figure data-orig-width="798" data-orig-height="596" class="tmblr-full"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_orhcw1UMXs1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-width="798" data-orig-height="596" /></figure>
<p>21 May – Chicago Swing Katz.<br />
Playing music from the jazz age to the swing craze and in the Chicago<br />
style were musicians Jeff Matthews (reeds), Pete Ainge (trumpet), Mike Owen<br />
(trombone), Roy Sainsbury (guitar), Derek Harrison (piano), Mike Carnie (drums)<br />
and Edward Lloyd-Hughes (double bass).  A<br />
power cut affected electric instruments and put the lights out so instead of<br />
jazz in the park we had ‘jazz in the dark&rsquo;.<br />
Initially non-plussed for ten seconds the band valliantly played on.</p>
<figure data-orig-width="807" data-orig-height="602" class="tmblr-full"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_orhcwdrfT51rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-width="807" data-orig-height="602" /></figure>
<p>14 May – The Wabash Jazzmen.    This was the second of two Stafford Music<br />
Festival concerts that we managed to arrange.<br />
Hot, driving jazz in a distinctive rhythmic, foot-tapping style led by<br />
Mark Challinor on banjo, with Gordon Whitworth (trumpet), Mike Pembroke<br />
(trombone), Mike Hayler (reeds) and Richard Vernon (double bass).  This band really packs a punch and was well<br />
received.</p>
<figure data-orig-width="810" data-orig-height="605" class="tmblr-full"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_orhcx5bcKc1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-width="810" data-orig-height="605" /></figure>
<p>7 May – The Pedigree Jazzmen.  The first of two Stafford Music Festival<br />
concerts they gave us ‘A Salute to Trad Jazz&rsquo; with music from the 50s and<br />
60s.  Chris Walker led on clarinet, with<br />
Tony Mann (trombone), Andy Chislett (trumpet), John Noddings (banjo/guitar),<br />
Ken Ames (double bass) and Colin Larn (drums).<br />
Great music, great playing, great concert.</p>
<figure data-orig-width="818" data-orig-height="611" class="tmblr-full"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_orhcxmVctv1rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-width="818" data-orig-height="611" /></figure>
<p>30 April – Christine Tyrrell with Matt Palmer&rsquo;s Rhythmic<br />
Reeds.  Such a foot-tapping<br />
performance.  Matt Palmer always gives<br />
101%.  Richard Vernon was sitting in for<br />
Brian Lawrence on the bass,k Mark Challinor (guitar/banjo), George Huxley<br />
(reeds) and Nick Ward (drums).  Christine<br />
Tyrrell was in good voice and is always welcome at the White Eagle Club.</p>
<figure data-orig-width="819" data-orig-height="611" class="tmblr-full"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/tumblr/tumblr_inline_orhcybJxH91rp3l2g_540.webp" data-orig-width="819" data-orig-height="611" /></figure>
<p>23 April – Richard Leach&rsquo;s Clubhouse 6.  This was last SJS evening concert as this has<br />
now been discontinued.  Richard and his<br />
band were in great form and played with a richness and vibrancy delightful to<br />
hear.   There was a good range from fast<br />
and furious, through soulful to gentle.<br />
Band consisted of Richard Leach (trombone), Gordon Whitworth (trumpet),<br />
George Huxley (reeds), Brian Mellor (banjo + guitar), John Fellows (bass +<br />
sousaphone) and Nick Ward (drums).</p>
