<p>And others who bash</p>
<p>They do it for bucks</p>
<p>And they do it for cash.</p>
<p>They love the applause</p>
<p>And the moments of glory</p>
<p>But sometimes forget</p>
<p>The whole point of the story.</p>
<p>It’s the music that matters</p>
<p>And the good folk who strive</p>
<p>Without praise, without profit</p>
<p>To keep it alive.</p>
<p>So let’s drink a toast</p>
<p>‘May the god of jazz bless</p>
<p> My friend Mr Balmforth</p>
<p>And the old SJS’.</p>
<p><i>A poetic message<br />
to Stafford Jazz Society</i></p>
<p><i> from the Society’s President</i></p>
<p><i> Band Leader and Jazz Broadcaster</i></p>
<p><b><i>Mel Hill</i></b></p>
